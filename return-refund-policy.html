01 — OVERVIEW

Our Return Philosophy

At ÉLAN and MITRA, we want you to love what you wear. If for any reason you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we are here to help — within the terms outlined in this policy.

We follow a 7-day return window from the date of delivery, in line with standard Indian e-commerce practices and consumer protection guidelines.

Note: All return requests must be raised within 7 days of the delivery date. Requests raised after this period will not be accepted under any circumstances.

02 — ELIGIBILITY

What Can Be Returned?

Returns are accepted only if the following conditions are met:

  • Item is unused, unwashed, and unworn
  • All original tags, labels, and packaging are intact
  • Item is returned within 7 days of delivery
  • Return request is raised via email or WhatsApp with unboxing video / photos as proof
  • Item is not from the non-returnable category listed below

⚠️ Items without original packaging, tags removed, or showing signs of use/wash will not be accepted and will be returned to the customer at their expense.

04 — NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS

Items Not Eligible for Return

The following items are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable under any circumstances:

  • Items purchased during sale, discount, or promotional events
  • Customised or altered garments (stitched to measurements)
  • Dupattas, scarves, and accessories for hygiene reasons
  • Items that have been worn, washed, dry-cleaned, or altered by the customer
  • Items with missing tags, labels, or original packaging
  • Items reported after the 7-day return window
  • Gift cards and store credit vouchers

MITRA handcrafted ethnic wear (lehengas, sarees) is returnable only in case of a manufacturing defect or incorrect item delivery, not for personal preference or sizing concerns. We recommend checking our size guide before purchase.

05 — RETURN PROCESS

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Raise a request — Email us at info@elanmitra.com within 7 days of delivery. Include your order number, reason for return, and clear photos/unboxing video of the item.
  2. Approval — Our team will review your request within 48 business hours and confirm eligibility. We reserve the right to reject returns that do not meet our policy conditions.
  3. Pack securely — Pack the item in its original packaging with all tags intact. Write your order ID on the outside of the parcel.
  4. Ship the item — For approved returns, we will share the return shipping address. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective or incorrectly delivered.
  5. Inspection — Once we receive the item, it will be inspected within 3–5 business days. Items not meeting our return conditions will be sent back to the customer.
  6. Refund / Exchange — Approved refunds or exchanges will be processed within 7–10 business days of successful inspection.
06 — REFUNDS

Refund Methods & Timeline

Refunds are processed as follows depending on the payment method used:

  • UPI / Net Banking / Debit Card: Refund to original account within 7–10 business days
  • Credit Card: Refund to original card within 7–10 business days (may take 1–2 billing cycles depending on your bank)
  • Store Credit: Credited to your account within 48 hours — valid for 12 months from date of issue
  • COD Orders: Refund via NEFT/bank transfer only. Customer must provide bank details at time of return request.

⚠️ Original shipping charges are non-refundable. If your order qualified for free shipping, a standard shipping fee of ₹99 will be deducted from your refund amount.

07 — DAMAGED / WRONG ITEMS

Received a Defective or Wrong Item?

We sincerely apologise if you have received a damaged, defective, or incorrect item. In such cases:

  • Report within 48 hours of delivery with photos and/or unboxing video
  • We will arrange a free pickup and send a replacement at no extra cost
  • If a replacement is not available, a full refund will be issued to your original payment method

We strongly recommend recording an unboxing video for every order. This is the easiest way to resolve any disputes quickly and ensures your claim is processed without delay.

08 — ORDER CANCELLATIONS

Cancellation Policy

  • Before dispatch: Orders can be cancelled within 12 hours of placing the order. Full refund will be issued.
  • After dispatch: Orders cannot be cancelled once shipped. Please initiate a return after delivery.
  • Custom/altered orders: Cannot be cancelled once production has begun.

To cancel an order, email us immediately at info@elanmitra.com with your order number.
