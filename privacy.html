Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 14 October 2026

📅 Last updated: 14 October 2026  |  This policy applies to elanmitra.com

1. Who We Are

MITRA is an Indian ethnic saree brand operated by Elan Mitra, selling handcrafted silk sarees online at elanmitra.com. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at info@elanmitra.com.

2. Information We Collect

When you place an order or interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal details — your name, email address and phone number
  • Delivery address — street address, city, state and PIN code
  • Payment information — we use Razorpay to process payments; we do not store your card details on our servers
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent on site, browser type (collected via Google Analytics if enabled)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfil your saree orders
  • Send order confirmation and delivery updates to your email
  • Respond to your queries and customer support requests
  • Improve our website and product offerings
  • Send occasional offers or new collection announcements (you can opt out anytime)

4. Payment Processing

All payments on elanmitra.com are securely processed by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We do not store, see or have access to your credit card, debit card or UPI details. Razorpay's privacy policy can be viewed at razorpay.com/privacy.

5. Order Notifications

We use Formspree to send order notification emails to our team. Your order details (name, address, items ordered) are transmitted securely through Formspree solely for the purpose of processing your order.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect some website functionality.

If Google AdSense ads are displayed on our blog, Google may use cookies to show you relevant advertisements based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

7. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your delivery details with our logistics/courier partner solely to fulfil your order. We will not share your information with any other party without your consent, except where required by Indian law.

8. Data Retention

We retain your order information for a period of 3 years for accounting and legal compliance purposes. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us at info@elanmitra.com.

9. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@elanmitra.com and we will respond within 7 business days.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Our website uses HTTPS encryption to secure data transmitted between your browser and our server. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
